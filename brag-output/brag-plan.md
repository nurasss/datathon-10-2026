# Brag Plan: ForteVision

## Nine-question rubric
1. Local CPU analysis of cashier-area .mov recordings, identifying visual signs of possible violations and mapping events to a curator-approved registry of Kazakhstan regulations.
2. Video stays local; evidence is actual source frames, not model-generated imagery or invented citations.
3. A blue event marker turns an otherwise anonymous recording into an inspectable timestamp.
4. Recreate the real upload form and processing banner from apps/api/app/ui.py, then show an editorial enlargement of the result fields. Do not expose private recordings or keys.
5. 20 seconds, four scenes.
6. Polished: a precise evidence-first datathon product film.
7. Steady instrumental bed, two restrained impact accents and one UI click. No narration.
8. Built ForteVision: from cashier-area video to timestamped visual evidence and checked regulatory references. Local processing. A human makes the final call.
9. Upload .mov → local processing → inspect timestamp, evidence and regulatory source. Demo timing is compressed, not a performance benchmark.

## Angle and hook
“Не просто запись. Проверяемое событие.” Turn a recording into something a reviewer can inspect. No claims of universal detection, legal certainty or production readiness.

## Identity and format
1920×1080 landscape, 20s. Background #0f1115, panels #171a21 / #1e222b, text #e8eaf0, muted #9aa3b2, accent #2f7cf6. Arial regular/bold as a locally bundled match for the source system-sans UI. Oversized editorial titles, source-faithful dark UI.
